      What this is, and what it isn’t

      • It is a routing suggestion tool built on clearly-labeled synthetic cases.
      • It is not a reporting system. Nothing you type, text, or speak is submitted anywhere.
      • It is not a legal determination of who is responsible. A person at the agency confirms that.
      • It does not use your location, look up boundaries, or call anyone — including 911.
      • It stores nothing. Your answers live in this browser tab and vanish when you reload.
      • The text path is simulated. The short code is not a live number and no message is sent.
